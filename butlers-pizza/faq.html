FAQ's

Can you order pizza by the slice?

No, but we offer 7-inch personal-size pizzas that are a great option for one person.

Do you deliver?

We do not deliver directly. Check Butler's Pizza on DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub.

Do you cater?

Yes. We deliver catering orders of $100 or more. We deliver to your location, and you will need to serve your guests.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday–Sunday from 11:00 am–8:00 pm.

What if I want a catering order outside your normal business hours?

Email 3dbutlerpizza@att.net, and we will be happy to discuss options for your catering event.

Can I call in an order?

Yes. Call us at 317-600-5463 or 317-600-8793.

Can I order online?

Yes. Order online here.

Do you have social media?

Yes. Find us on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Yelp. You can also find us on Google.

Do you offer discounts?

We run specials on our Instagram page and offer coupons on our website.

What specials do you have right now?

Please check our website or social media pages for current specials.

How much are your pizzas?

Please review our online ordering platform for current pricing.

How much are your specialty pizzas?

Specialty pizzas start at $35.

Are you hiring? Where can I fill out an application?

We are always accepting applications.

Do you offer gluten-free crust?

We offer gluten-free or cauliflower crust for an additional $4–$5. These crusts are available in 10-inch only.

What ingredients can I add to stuffed breadsticks?

You can add any ingredient you would add to a pizza, with up to three toppings. Two toppings are included in the price; the third is an additional charge.

Do you offer lunch specials?

Yes. Our lunch specials run from 11:00 am–2:00 pm.
